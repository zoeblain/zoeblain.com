Bio and CV
Zoe Blain is an artist, writer and editor working on Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung and Bunurong land. She makes prints, codes and collects things, including screenshots, computer parts and found text from the internet. She also on the committee at Trocadero Projects. Zoe's influences include the blue screen of death, hard garbage piles and the vast network of fibre optic cables that stretch across the ocean. ----------Group exhibitions---------- * Flex Disket, GalleryGalleryInc, 2026 * Spectrum, George Paton Gallery, 2025 * Sēmeíōsis, Melbourne Design Week, MPavillion Parkville, George Paton Gallery and Practice Lab, 2025 * Cut it Out, The Old Bar, 2016 * Dark Side of the Docks, Food Court Art Space, 2016 * The Full Picture, Studio 15, Nicholas Building, 2014 ---------Recent publications--------- * 'Continuing Until Further Notice', no purchase issue 3: debt, 2026 * 'semiosis.py', sidewalk syntax, 2026 --------------Education-------------- * Machine Language, School for Poetic Computation, 2026 * Graduate Certificate of Visual Art, University of Melbourne (VCA), 2024–2025 * User Interface Design, RMIT University, 2021 * User Experience Design, RMIT University, 2021 * Bachelor of Media and Communication (Honours), RMIT University, 2016 * Bachelor of Arts, (Creative Writing), RMIT University, 2012–2014 * Certificate II in Interactive Digital Media, 2010–2011
